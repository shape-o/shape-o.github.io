The Great Collapse: Origins and Structural Failure

Published August 2026 · Investigative Desk

Who Created the Ride?

Originally constructed in the early decades of amusement park history, this legendary wooden roller coaster was designed to push the boundaries of engineering with steep drops and sharp wooden turns.

Cause of the Collapse

According to structural engineers, decades of severe weathering combined with rot in the foundational timber supports led to a catastrophic failure. Once the core load-bearing beams gave way, the entire framework imploded inward onto itself.
